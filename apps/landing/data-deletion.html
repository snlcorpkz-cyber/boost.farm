Data Deletion Instructions

This page explains how to request the deletion of your personal data collected by the BoostFarm application (the "Application"), created by MANYBOOST FZCO (the "Service Provider").

What data we store

When you use BoostFarm we may store the following data tied to your account:

  • Account identifiers (email address, authentication tokens, device identifier)
  • Game progress, in-game currency balance, farm state, offer completions, ad-watch history
  • Technical and analytics data: IP address, device model, operating system version, install attribution parameters
How to request deletion

To have all of your personal data permanently deleted, please follow the steps below:

  1. Send an email to hello@boostfarm.io from the email address associated with your BoostFarm account.
  2. Use the subject line: Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address you used to sign in to the Application. If you remember them, add any additional identifiers (in-game nickname, approximate registration date).
We will process your request within 30 calendar days. You will receive a confirmation email once your data has been permanently erased from our systems.
What gets deleted

Upon a verified deletion request we erase:

  • Your account record and all linked game progress
  • All events, transactions and attribution records that can identify you as an individual
  • Cached authentication tokens and push notification subscriptions
What may be retained

Some records may be retained for a limited period where required by law or for legitimate business needs (e.g. anti-fraud, financial reconciliation, compliance with postback requirements of our advertising partners). Such data is kept in an anonymized form that cannot identify you personally.

Alternative: uninstall the app

You can stop all further collection of information by the Application at any time by uninstalling it using the standard uninstall process on your device. Uninstalling the app does not by itself delete data that has already been sent to our servers — for that you need to follow the email request process above.

Contact

If you have any questions about this process, please contact us at hello@boostfarm.io.
